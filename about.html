ABOUT

Outpost 31 is an independent production studio working across sound, music and video for games, screen and creative projects.

The studio handles practical creative work from early ideas through to production, post-production, finishing and delivery. That can include music, sound design, recording, editing, mixing, video production, production support and problem solving.

It is run by Julian Costa, with more than 20 years of hands-on experience across broadcast, sound, video, music, post-production and teaching.

Outpost 31 is a working production studio space in Mt Lawley, Western Australia, on Whadjuk Noongar Country.
