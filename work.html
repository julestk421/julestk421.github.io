WORK WITH ME

Outpost 31 is a small production studio working across sound, music and video for games, screen and creative projects.

I can help at different stages of a project. Early, during production, or near the finish line: turning an idea into something workable, recording or creating new material, editing what already exists, preparing things for release, or helping work out what the project needs next.

Bring me an idea, build, clip, trailer, rough edit, brief or odd production problem. I'm good at joining the dots between sound, music, video, technical details and finding the practical way through.

If your project needs sound, music, video production, post-production, polish, or help getting into shape, send it through.
