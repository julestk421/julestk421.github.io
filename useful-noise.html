USEFUL NOISE

Useful Noise is where I record things that caught my ear while working.

Old machines, odd instruments, broken electronics, mechanical clunks, strange textures, and sounds that are easy to miss until you keep an ear out for them.

I put a microphone near them, and now you can discover some interesting sounds too.

Some of them become useful for games, films, music or sound design. Some are just satisfying to listen to.

Follow along on YouTube or Instagram.
